## Primary — `photography and videography` (90/mo · KD 12 · CPC $120)

**Why this term:**
The homepage's only SEO job is to own the multi-service framing — photo AND video — without competing with any spoke. This is the only term that accurately names both disciplines together, carries hire-adjacent intent (CPC $120), and has a SERP that a portfolio site can actually enter.

**SERP confirms attainability.** The top-10 CA SERP is fragmented and low-DR across its organic positions. Studio Lumen (Calgary) holds position 2 at DR 34; Lewis & Co. (Calgary wedding) holds position 3 at DR 24; Black + White (Canmore studio) holds position 6 at DR 14 — all are freelance/small-studio portfolio sites drawing real traffic. Reddit and Quora fill several spots with discussion content. There is no authoritative aggregator or agency locking down this SERP; the pattern rewards a dedicated portfolio homepage with genuine topical breadth.

**Geo-personalisation upside.** Like most non-branded creatives SERPs, results are geo-weighted. A London, ON homepage with local signals should surface disproportionately to SW Ontario searches, similar to the advantage identified for the wedding spoke pages.

**No cannibalization.** The photography hub (`/photography`) targets `photographer near me`. The video hub (`/videos`) targets `videographer`. Every spoke targets its specific service. No page in the current or planned structure targets the combined "photography and videography" framing — the homepage is the natural and only right owner.

**Suggested h1 framing:**
> "Photography & Videography — Sharp by Tony"

or as a more personal brand signal:
> "Sharp by Tony — Photography, Video & Design"

Either naturally incorporates the primary and leaves room for design as a third signal without keyword-diluting the h1.

## Secondaries

### `content creation services` (250/mo · KD 3 · CPC $110)

Commercial intent confirmed (Ahrefs flags both `informational` and `commercial`). At 250/mo this is the highest-volume term in the safe candidate set and carries a CPC that confirms advertiser belief in hire intent.

**SERP reality check — do not target as primary.** The CA SERP for this term is occupied by high-DR agency directories (Semrush directory DR 92 at position 2, Brafton DR 79 at position 4, Siege Media DR 79 at position 5). The lowest-DR organic result is Vyews.ca at DR 24 in position 8. The structural fit is agency/directory, not individual portfolio — a KD of 3 understates the actual difficulty for a solo creative. Targeting it as a homepage primary would waste the h1. As a b1 secondary it adds commercial framing without claiming a ranking fight the site can't win yet.

**Use in b1:** naturally fits language like "… offering content creation services across photo, video, and brand design."

### `media production` (300/mo · KD 7 · CPC $120)

Highest raw volume in the safe set at 300/mo. Intent skews informational, but CPC $120 confirms commercial adjacency and its use in b1 signals breadth of offering without specificity. No spoke or hub targets it. Use as positioning language rather than a ranking target in its own right.

**Use in b1:** "… a London, Ontario media production studio offering photography, videography, and design."

### `photography videography` (30/mo · KD 0 · CPC $80)

The condensed phonetic variant of the primary. KD 0 means zero competition — a page ranking for the primary will almost certainly rank for this automatically. Include it as natural copy variation in the b1 rather than forcing it; Google will close the gap.

## Implementation notes

**h1** — target the primary. One clean headline that names the brand and the combined photo + video offering. No need to stuff secondaries here.

**b1 (subtitle / hero paragraph)** — one or two sentences. Weave in `content creation services` and/or `media production` as natural positioning language. Include `photography videography` only if copy flows to it; don't force it.

**What the homepage is NOT doing:** it is not trying to rank for any individual service (wedding, corporate, headshot, etc.). Those are the spokes' jobs. The homepage ranks for the brand and the multi-service positioning; it then routes visitors to the relevant spoke via internal links.

**Internal linking priority:** hero CTAs and nav links to `/photography`, `/videos`, and the highest-traffic spokes (wedding photo, headshot, real estate, corporate video) are more valuable for overall site SEO than any additional keyword the homepage might target. The homepage's equity distribution matters more than its own ranking once the spokes are live.
