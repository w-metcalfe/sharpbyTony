## Photography pages

### Hub — `/photography`
- **Primary:** `photographer near me` (1,000 / KD 7)
- **Secondary:** `freelance photographer` (250 / KD 11) · `hire a photographer` (70 / KD 2) · `photography services` (150 / KD 28) · `professional photographer` (250 / KD 9)

## Cross-cutting notes for content execution

- **Educational-SERP pages need transactional framing.** `portrait photography`, `product photography`, and `food photography` all have top-10s dominated by tutorials and explainers. Each spoke must lead with deliverables, packages, and CTAs on the first screen — not technique content — so Google reads them as service pages, not guides. `food photographer` (not `food photography`) is intentionally the food primary for the same reason.
- **Local qualifiers punch above weight.** `wedding photographer london ontario` (50 / KD 6) and `wedding videographer london ontario` (20 / KD 2) are low-volume nationally but high-value for SW Ontario via geo-personalisation. Weave into copy rather than targeting as primaries.
- **"Near me" modifiers don't justify separate URLs.** Outside the hub primaries, no `[service] near me` modifier cracked 100/mo for any spoke. Local-pack signals are better served by Google Business Profile optimisation than separate URL targeting at this stage.
- **Toronto modifier on video corporate/wedding** — `corporate videographer toronto` (350 / KD 18) and `wedding videographer toronto` (200 / KD 14) match the primary in volume but are explicitly geo-locked. Embedding in supporting copy is preferable to targeting as primaries from a London, ON base.
- **Wedding photo ↔ wedding video cross-link.** `wedding photographer and videographer` (150 / KD 24) signals bundled-service demand. Both wedding pages should prominently cross-link.
- **Photo hub vs. spokes priority.** Hub seed terms (`photographer` KD 67 · `photography` KD 82) are not near-term winnable. The hub is internal-linking architecture; the spokes drive actual lead traffic.
- **Boudoir** — deferred per user direction; revisit as a separate decision once spoke pages are scoped.
- **Construction photo and video** — low search volume across all CA terms (see section below). Both pages are primarily portfolio/brand-credibility plays, not traffic drivers. SEO is a secondary return on these pages.

## Construction pages

> **Volume reality check.** Construction-specific search terms are genuinely thin in Canada. The highest-volume construction photography term nationally is `construction progress photography` at 80/mo; the construction video cluster peaks at 30/mo. This is an expected characteristic of a B2B trade niche — construction firms hire photographers and videographers through referrals, LinkedIn, and portfolio reviews, not Google search. Both pages are worth building for portfolio credibility, topical authority within the commercial cluster, and the occasional inbound lead — but neither should be projected as a meaningful organic traffic driver in the near term.

### Construction photography

#### Suggested FAQ questions

> Ordered by commercial intent (highest-intent questions first). Questions only — no answers at this stage.

1. How much does construction photography cost in Canada?
2. What types of construction photography do you offer — progress documentation, completion shots, drone/aerial, or team on-site?
3. Can you photograph an active job site safely, and what do you need from us to coordinate access?
4. Do you offer ongoing progress photography packages for multi-phase or long-duration builds?
5. Can drone or aerial photography be added to a construction shoot?
6. How are the final photos delivered, and what usage rights do we get for marketing, permitting, and social media?

> **Research note:** `construction photography rates` (20/mo) and `construction photo documentation` (10/mo) are the only question-signal terms with measurable CA volume — the cluster is too niche for Ahrefs to surface full question-match data. Questions 1, 3, 4, and 6 are grounded directly in those signals; questions 2 and 5 are inferred from the `aerial construction photography` cluster (40/mo) and the B2B buyer's standard pre-hire concerns.

- **Primary:** `construction photography` (50 / KD 0)
- **Secondary:** `construction progress photography` (80 / KD n/a) · `construction site photography` (30 / KD 0) · `construction photographer` (10 / KD 0) · `industrial photography` (100 / KD 0) · `aerial construction photography` (40 / KD n/a) · `construction drone photography` (20 / KD n/a)
- **Rationale:** `construction photography` is the most direct hire-intent term with confirmed KD 0 and a SERP that a low-authority page can actually enter — position 1 is held by robertlowdon.com at DR 33 with just 68 backlinks; position 6 is mkfoto.ca at DR 9. No aggregator or stock site owns the commercial layer. `construction progress photography` (80/mo) is the highest-volume term in the cluster and belongs in the copy/H2s even though Ahrefs has no SERP or KD data for it yet — it signals the documentation use case (developer/contractor progress docs) that is likely Tony's most common construction client scenario. `industrial photography` (100/mo, KD 0) is the best-volume adjacent term and has a similarly weak commercial SERP (Robert Lowdon ranks #1 with an informational post at DR 33; a dedicated service page would be an anomaly and get rewarded). Include it in copy and meta to pull both the construction and broader industrial client audience.
