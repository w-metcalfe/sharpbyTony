## Cross-cluster execution notes

- **Cross-link wedding photo ↔ wedding video** — `wedding photographer and videographer` (150 / KD 24) signals bundled demand that neither page can capture alone.
- **Cross-link commercial photo ↔ event photo ↔ corporate video** — all share the B2B buyer; mutual linking compounds topical authority.
- **Educational-SERP discipline** — product, portrait/family, and (food, on the seed term) require deliverables + CTA above the fold to read as service pages, not guides.
- **Local qualifiers in-copy, not as primaries** — London, ON and Toronto modifiers belong in supporting copy. Google Business Profile carries the "near me" load better than separate URLs at this stage.
- **Boudoir deferred** — 3,400/mo, KD 0 is the largest single photo term in the dataset but is held out per user direction. Revisit as a standalone decision.
- **Photo hub vs. spokes** — the spokes carry near-term lead traffic; the hub's near-term job is internal-link architecture. Plan link equity flow accordingly.
