# Baseline Keyword Research — Photo & Video Service Pages

**Geographic target:** Canada (national)
**Data source:** Ahrefs (Keywords Explorer + SERP Overview), country filter CA
**Scope:** structural recommendation only — no URLs, headings, meta titles, or content briefs

## Summary

**Photography → hub + spokes (strong signal).** The photo SERPs consistently reward niche, audience-specific pages over generalist ones, and the volume distribution is broad and well-clustered (wedding, commercial/brand, product, headshot, real estate, portrait/family, food). A single "photography" page cannot rank for these simultaneously; the broad seed terms (`photographer` KD 67, `photography` KD 82) are also too authority-heavy to be near-term targets.

**Video → hub + spokes (also strong, but for a different reason).** The broad term `videographer` (1,400/mo, KD 0) has a surprisingly weak commercial SERP (YouTube, Reddit, Job Bank, Wikipedia) and is genuinely open, but `video production company` (500/mo, KD 41) is locked down by Toronto agency homepages and directories (Clutch, ProductionHub). Niche terms (corporate, wedding, event, music) consistently show low-DR dedicated pages ranking. A hub captures the open broad term; spokes go where the niche-page pattern is the SERP norm.

Both services need a hub + spokes. Photo gets more spokes than video, reflecting its broader cluster distribution.

### Natural clusters

- **Wedding/events** — dominant by raw volume. Seed term 1,200/mo plus city variants (Toronto, Vancouver, Ottawa, Calgary, Montreal) push the cluster to ~5,000–6,000/mo CA. Engagement, elopement, proposal extend it.
- **Commercial / brand / product** — second-largest hire-intent cluster. Real estate (1,600), product (1,100), commercial (~700 combined), food (~1,200). KD 0–13 throughout — thin specialist competition.
- **Portrait / headshot** — headshot photo (1,200), portrait photography (1,600), actor headshots (400), headshots toronto (600). Mixed personal/professional intent.
- **Family / life milestones** — newborn (1,100), maternity (200), family (200), boudoir (3,400, KD 0). B2C personal hire.
- **Education / technique** — very high volume (rule of thirds 2,100, leading lines 1,500) but zero hire intent. Filter out.

### SERP composition

- **"wedding photographer"** — local pack first; positions 2–3 are Reddit and WeddingWire.ca (DR 78); organic 5–9 are exclusively geo-specific pages ("Regina Wedding Photography," etc.). No national hub page ranks top 10. *Niche + local pages win; directories own the broad national query.*
- **"commercial photographer"** — local pack first; organic 3–5 are individual photographer service pages at DR 2–16 outranking Upwork (DR 91) and Bark.com (DR 88). *Most winnable SERP in the set.*
- **"product photography"** — AI overview + educational dominance (Adobe.ca DR 96, Reddit, YouTube, BigCommerce). *Service pages do not rank; local pack captures hire intent.*
- **"headshot photographer"** — local pack + low-DR (1–27) individual service pages. actorheadshots.ca (Toronto, DR 17) at position 7 — niche audience pages win.
- **"event photography"** — split intent; Eventective directory, Format.com guide, Adobe tips; hire intent captured locally.

### Recommendation — hub + 8 spokes (ranked by opportunity)

1. **Wedding photography** — highest volume, KD 5–13, individual pages do rank
2. **Commercial / brand photography** — KD 0 on both seed terms, low-DR pages outrank marketplaces
3. **Headshot photography** — ~2,000/mo combined, niche pages win, actor/corporate sub-segments viable
4. **Real estate photography** — 1,600/mo, KD 13, CPC $1.60 (strong advertiser signal)
5. **Product photography** — 1,100/mo but requires explicitly transactional framing to avoid educational SERP
6. **Portrait / family photography** — 1,600 + 200/mo, B2C hire
7. **Food photography** — 1,000/mo; SERP is informational today but valuable for topical authority and restaurant/hospitality cross-sell
8. **Event photography** — 400/mo, KD 9, supports the commercial cluster

The hub page itself targets `photographer` / `photography` for brand positioning. Its near-term role is internal linking architecture, not direct ranking — those seed terms (KD 67/82) are not winnable in the near term.

### Natural clusters

- **Tier 1 — broad gateway terms (800–1,400/mo):** `videographer`, `videography`, `video production` — mixed intent (hire + informational + jobs).
- **Tier 2 — corporate/B2B + wedding + event (300–500/mo):** `video production company`, `corporate video production`, `corporate videographer toronto`, `video production agency`, `event videography`, `wedding videographer`.
- **Tier 3 — niche specialties (100–200/mo):** music video, brand/social/marketing, product/commercial, explainer/training, real estate video.

Total commercial volume is modest vs. global but consistent with the Canadian freelance market. Outside the broad seeds, no single niche breaks 500/mo.

### SERP composition

- **"videographer"** — YouTube, Vidico pricing listicle, Wikipedia, Reddit "how to become a videographer," Canada Job Bank, WeddingWire SK at position 6. Almost no true commercial/portfolio content ranking. *Genuinely open commercial opportunity.*
- **"video production company"** — Clutch.co (DR 91, pos 2), ProductionHub (DR 73, pos 6), plus Toronto agency homepages (Key West Video, Sailfin, Twistedframe, Minifridge, SVG). *Locked down; a non-Toronto freelancer cannot crack this.*
- **"corporate video production"** — Clutch + AI FAQ + low-DR Canadian agency dedicated pages (e.g. originfilms.ca DR 20) ranking with real traffic. *Dedicated corporate-video pages win, not broad homepages — attainable.*
- **"wedding videographer"** — heavy geo-personalisation; WeddingWire (DR 78), Eventective (DR 75), low-DR niche local videographers. *Local niche pages slot into the pattern; a London, ON page should surface to SW Ontario searchers.*
- **"event videography"** — KD 3 plus zero-KD adjacents indicate low competition consistent with the wedding pattern.

### Recommendation — hub + 4 spokes (ranked by opportunity)

1. **Corporate / brand video** — corporate video production (350, KD 12) + brand video (100, KD 1) + marketing video production (150, KD 0) + commercial video production services (100, KD 1). Highest CPC cluster ($300–$1,100); low KD; dedicated low-DR pages already rank.
2. **Wedding videography** — wedding videographer (300, KD 0) + bundled-hire variants. Geo-personalised SERP means a local, niche page is the right shape.
3. **Event video** — event videography (350, KD 3) + event video production (150, KD 0). Very low competition; complements corporate and wedding.
4. **Music video production** — 150/mo, KD 3, distinct intent, Tony has direct portfolio proof. A single dedicated page can own it without cluster overlap.

Defer to post-launch: product video, explainer/training, real estate video — each 60–100/mo and would need portfolio content to support credibly.

The hub page captures the broad open seed terms (`videographer`, `videography`, `video production services`) and serves as the parent linking to each spoke.

## Recommended page structure

| Service | Structure | Spokes (priority order) |
|---|---|---|
| **Photography** | Hub + spokes | wedding · commercial/brand · headshot · real estate · product · portrait/family · food · event |
| **Video** | Hub + spokes | corporate/brand · wedding · event · music video |

**Reasoning, tied to evidence:**

- **Photo evidence** — three independent SERPs (`wedding photographer`, `commercial photographer`, `headshot photographer`) showed low-DR niche / audience-specific pages outranking high-DR generalists and marketplaces. Volume is also distributed across 5+ commercial clusters, none of which a single page could target without thinning relevance. The hub seed terms (`photographer` KD 67 · `photography` KD 82) are not near-term winnable, so the hub's near-term value is internal-linking architecture and brand framing.
- **Video evidence** — the broad term (`videographer` 1,400/mo, KD 0) has a weak, fragmented commercial SERP and is winnable by the hub itself. Conversely, `video production company` (KD 41) is owned by Toronto agency homepages and Clutch/ProductionHub directories — a freelancer hub cannot displace them, so the spokes need to be specialty-shaped (corporate, wedding, event, music) where the SERP rewards niche pages. Fewer spokes than photo because volume thins out below 200/mo for most other video niches.

## Open questions / next steps

1. **CA-city modifiers** — London, ON · Toronto · Hamilton · KW are obvious local layers. A follow-up should pull `[service] [city]` permutations and decide whether to (a) embed location signals in each spoke, or (b) build separate location pages, or (c) hybrid.
2. **Competitor gap analysis** — pull top-ranking pages for the priority spokes (e.g. `corporate video production` low-DR winners; `commercial photographer` low-DR winners) and map their content depth, length, schema, internal linking. Establishes the bar a Sharp by Tony spoke needs to clear.
3. **Bundled-service intent** — `wedding photographer and videographer` (150/mo, KD 24) is a real combo query. Decide whether wedding-photo and wedding-video spokes cross-link prominently, or whether a bundled "wedding" spoke makes sense.
4. **Product photography intent split** — the SERP is fully educational. A follow-up should test whether a transactional spoke can rank, or whether the right play is a hybrid guide + service page.
5. **Real estate video** is a small but high-intent niche (60–100/mo); worth a quick second look once the four primary video spokes are scoped.
6. **Authority pacing** — the hub seed terms (`photographer` / `photography`) are authority-heavy. Worth deciding when (not whether) to start linkbuilding against them, given the hub will not rank near-term regardless of on-page work.
7. **Validate boudoir cluster** — 3,400/mo, KD 0 is the single highest-volume photo term in the set. Confirm whether this is a service Tony offers / wants to position around before treating it as a spoke candidate.
