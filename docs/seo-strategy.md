# SEO Strategy — Sharp by Tony

**Geographic target:** Canada (national), London ON / SW Ontario emphasis
**Last updated:** 2026-06-21
**Source research:** `docs/keyword-research/`

---

## Overview

The site uses a **hub-and-spoke architecture** across two service verticals: Photography and Video. The homepage owns the multi-service brand positioning. Hubs own broad gateway terms. Spokes capture niche, audience-specific service terms where low-DR dedicated pages consistently outrank high-DR generalists and marketplaces.

**Total pages planned:** 16 (1 homepage · 2 hubs · 13 spokes)

**Key insight from SERP analysis:** across every priority cluster, individual freelance/studio pages at DR 2–34 outrank directories (Upwork, Bark, Clutch, ProductionHub) and even high-DR educational sites. Authority is not the blocker — correct page shape and niche framing are.

## Homepage

**URL:** `/`
**Primary keyword:** `photography and videography` (90/mo · KD 12 · CPC $120)
**Secondary:** `content creation services` (250/mo · KD 3) · `media production` (300/mo · KD 7) · `photography videography` (30/mo · KD 0)

The homepage's only SEO job is the **multi-service brand framing** — photo AND video together. It does not compete with any hub or spoke. Its primary value to overall SEO is **link equity distribution**: hero CTAs and nav links flowing to hubs and top spokes matter more than the homepage's own ranking.

## Content Execution Rules

### Transactional framing discipline
Portrait, product, and food photography SERPs are dominated by educational content (YouTube, Adobe, Canon, Reddit). Service pages in these clusters **must** lead with deliverables, packages, and CTA above the fold — Google needs to read them as service pages, not guides. `food photographer` (not `food photography`) is intentionally the food primary for this reason.

### Local qualifiers: in-copy, not as primaries
`wedding photographer london ontario` (50/mo) and `wedding videographer london ontario` (20/mo) are low-volume nationally but high-value for SW Ontario via geo-personalisation. Weave into supporting copy. Google Business Profile carries the "near me" load better than separate location URLs at this stage.

### "Near me" modifiers
No spoke-level `[service] near me` term exceeds 100/mo. Include in copy; do not build separate URL targeting.

### Toronto corridor mentions
`corporate videographer toronto` (350 / KD 18) and `wedding videographer toronto` (200 / KD 14) are geo-locked terms worth embedding in supporting copy but not targeting as spoke primaries from a London, ON base.

## Cross-Linking Strategy

| Link pair | Why |
|---|---|
| Wedding photo ↔ Wedding video | `wedding photographer and videographer` (150 / KD 24) signals bundled demand neither page captures alone |
| Commercial photo ↔ Event photo ↔ Corporate video | Shared B2B buyer; mutual linking compounds topical authority |
| Photography hub → all photo spokes | Hub equity flows down; spokes send relevance signals up |
| Video hub → all video spokes | Same; hub is also a near-term ranking target itself |
| Homepage → both hubs + top spokes | Hero CTAs and nav links are the most valuable SEO asset on the homepage |

## FAQ Strategy

Every service page gets **6 FAQ questions** marked up with `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema. Questions are hire-intent only (cost, process, deliverables, timing, logistics, scope) — no technique or tutorial questions. The full question list is in [`docs/keyword-research/faq-recommendations.md`](keyword-research/faq-recommendations.md).

Target: ~40–80 word answers in Tony's voice, AI-overview-friendly. Cross-link FAQs between related spokes (wedding photo ↔ wedding video, commercial photo ↔ corporate video ↔ event photo).
