# FAQ Recommendations — Photo & Video Service Pages

**Geographic target:** Canada (national)
**Data source:** Ahrefs (Keywords Explorer — matching terms + search suggestions, country filter CA)
**Companion to:** [keyword-research-baseline.md](keyword-research-baseline.md) · [keyword-research-spokes.md](keyword-research-spokes.md) · [keyword-clusters.md](keyword-clusters.md)
**Scope:** 6 FAQ questions per service page (14 pages, 84 questions total). Questions only — answers to be written separately.

## Methodology

Two parallel research passes pulled question-format keywords from Ahrefs for the primary keyword of every photo and video service page. Candidates were filtered to **hire-intent** questions (cost, process, deliverables, timing, logistics, scope) and explicitly excluded pure technique/educational questions ("how to take a wedding photo", "how to film a music video"). For pages where the SERP is educationally dominated (product, portrait/family, food), the question selection leans hard into transactional framing per the spokes doc's guidance.

Final 6 per page were selected for:
- **Real hire-intent** (not tutorial/DIY)
- **Spread** across pricing, process, deliverables, timing, logistics, scope — not 6 variants of "how much"
- **Page positioning** consistent with the keyword research (e.g. food = hire intent, not technique)
- **AI-overview eligibility** — natural-language questions phrased the way buyers actually ask them

Most long-tail FAQ phrases register 0/mo in Ahrefs CA individually — this is normal; they aggregate into parent terms and earn impressions via People Also Ask, AI overviews, and long-tail clustering.
